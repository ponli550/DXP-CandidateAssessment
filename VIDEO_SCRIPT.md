# Video script, one narrator (about 440 spoken words, roughly 3.5 minutes; limit is the brief's, check the page, assume 5:00)

WARNING: do not skip the opener. At Hackerdorm the judges asked for "more on the problem" because the presenter went straight to the demo. Say the problem aloud, first, with no screen movement. Rehearse it twice with a timer.

Record over the PDF, scrolling to the page in brackets. Times are cues, not a stopwatch. If a take passes 4:45, cut words, do not speak faster.

**0:00 to 0:40. The problem (page 1)**  
"I am Irfan Ali. A company hires a front-line sales person. Three interview rounds, three interviewers, three different forms. The forms are outdated and do not test what the job now demands: reading a dashboard, working from data, deciding alone in the field. Months later the hire leaves, and the company pays for recruiting, onboarding and training again. It knows turnover is high. It cannot say why some hires succeed and others do not."

**0:40 to 1:10. The idea and what people see (pages 1 to 2)**  
"Our answer: the assessment gives evidence, not a verdict. The candidate takes one structured assessment on a phone, in English, Bahasa Malaysia or Mandarin. The hiring manager gets a one-page profile with strengths, development gaps and risks, each tied to the questions behind it. The manager still decides."

**1:10 to 2:00. Requirements and the test (pages 2 to 3)**  
"Before any question is written, HR and sales managers agree what the role needs, level by level, and which areas are critical and which can be trained. That is the role profile. The assessment covers six areas: numerical, logical, situational judgement, problem-solving, self-leadership and data literacy. Scoring is plain rules, no AI, so every score can be explained. Each question has one ID and three independently translated versions, and we check after the pilot that no version is harder."

**2:00 to 2:40. Gaps versus risks, and the dashboard (pages 3 to 4)**  
"A gap in a trainable area is a development gap. A shortfall in a critical area is a risk indicator. The rules are data the company can change. The dashboard compares candidates side by side. We deliberately show no single ranked score, because one number hides the very gaps you want to see."

**2:40 to 3:20. Employees and validation (pages 4 to 5)**  
"About ten current employees take the same assessment, so a candidate can be placed against a real range, with the group size shown. We will not claim it predicts success from ten people. We record every hire's outcome so the evidence grows. Validation covers scoring accuracy, which must be 100 percent, reliability, item quality, language equivalence and fairness, and the report says what is proven and what is not."

**3:20 to 3:50. Delivery, risks, close (pages 6 to 7)**  
"Ten weeks from 2 November, each week with a test it must pass. We pilot with 20 to 30 candidates and about 10 employees, and end with a validation report and handover. The biggest risk is that we are developers, not psychometricians, so we ask the company for an HR lead and, if available, an organisational psychologist. Thank you."
