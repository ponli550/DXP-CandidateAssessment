# Kickoff plan: Front-Line Sales Candidate Assessment (selected 2026-10-15)

Project: 10 weeks from Monday 2 Nov 2026. Week 10 ends Friday 8 Jan 2027.

## The calendar problem, decide this first

| Week | Dates | Plan says | Collision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 30 Nov to 4 Dec | Profiling and flag rules | Codex hackathon is Saturday 5 Dec. Do not scope a heavy week 5. |
| 8 | 21 to 25 Dec | Admin, export, pilot readiness | Christmas week. |
| 9 | 28 Dec to 1 Jan | Pilot with 20 to 30 candidates and about 10 employees | Year-end. 1 Jan is a public holiday. Managers and candidates are least available. |
| 10 | 4 to 8 Jan | Validation report, handover | Fine. |

The proposal puts the pilot in the worst week of the year. Fix in week 1, in writing:
- Move the employee sessions to week 7 (14 to 18 Dec). Employees are inside the company and easier to schedule, and it gives early item statistics.
- Run the candidate pilot from week 8 to the first days of week 10, with the report written in week 10.
- If the company cannot supply 20 to 30 real candidates in that window, use volunteers and say so in the validation report.
- Ask the company for its year-end shutdown dates now.

## Questions to send the company before 2 Nov

1. Who is the HR lead, the sales operations contact, and the person who signs off the role profile?
2. Which front-line roles are in scope: one role, or several channels and regions?
3. Can we have the current job description and the existing assessment forms?
4. What turnover and tenure data exists, anonymised? We want a baseline to compare against, for example how long leavers lasted.
5. What performance data exists for current employees, who owns it, and how often is it updated?
6. How will the roughly 10 employees be chosen, and how will their consent be collected?
7. How many real candidates a week will apply between weeks 8 and 10? Can they be asked to take the pilot?
8. Which language for which location? Simplified or Traditional Chinese?
9. Where may data be hosted, what is the retention rule, and who is the data protection contact?
10. Who can act as the reviewer for job analysis and item keys, an HR psychologist or similar? If nobody, confirm the fallback in the proposal is acceptable.
11. Which 4 to 6 managers can join the 2-hour week 1 workshop, including at least one strong and one average performer?
12. Year-end shutdown dates and public-holiday constraints.

## Team decisions before 2 Nov

- The three unnamed members: names, areas, experience. The proposal table still has `[Name]` rows.
- One person owns the item bank content. Without an owner it will not get written in three languages.
- Stack frozen: React and Tailwind, react-i18next, Node.js and Express, MySQL. Scoring engine as a pure module with tests. No changes after week 2.
- Repo, branch rules and weekly review with the company.

## Week 1 workshop (2 hours)

1. Describe a strong hire and a weak hire in your own words, with real examples (10 min each).
2. For each of the six areas: required level, 1 to 5, and critical or trainable (60 min).
3. Review the gap and risk rules on one fake candidate. Does the output match your instinct? (20 min)
4. Agree pilot gates, consent text and who sees what (20 min).

Output: the signed role profile. Nothing else in the plan is valid until it exists.

## Commitments the proposal made that now bind us

- Scoring accuracy 100% against hand-scored sheets. Build the test sheets in week 2, not week 9.
- 20 to 30 candidates and about 10 employees. Needs the company's access, so get it in writing in week 1.
- Three reviewed languages and a cross-language item check.
- A validation report that says what is not proven.
- Handover documents listed in section 12.
